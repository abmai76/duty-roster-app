Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Duty Roster Universal Scheduler ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do not have servers that receive your data.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (assignee names, task names, schedules, time entries, holidays, contacts, management information, and any attached photos) is stored locally on your own device using your device's built-in storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. When you voluntarily choose to attach a photo or logo, the file is read locally by your browser and stored only on your device — it is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Internet Access

The App can function fully offline. When used online, it only loads its own interface files. No data is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual entries, assignments, or entire projects at any time from within the App. Clearing your browser/app data or uninstalling the App also removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
